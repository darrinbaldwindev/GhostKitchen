# Ghost Kitchen Pilot Concept Evaluation v1

**Status:** Working framework  
**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Objective

Select one pilot food concept for the Ghost Kitchen Delivery Template using evidence rather than brand preference.

## Candidate families

The initial research set remains:

- burgers
- chicken
- Asian street food
- healthy/protein bowls
- loaded fries/sides
- dessert-focused concepts

These remain candidates, not approved concepts.

## Scoring dimensions

Score each candidate 1–5 and record evidence.

| Dimension | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Delivery resilience | Quality after realistic delivery time |
| Food economics | Ingredient cost and gross contribution potential |
| Prep complexity | Number and complexity of production steps |
| Labour efficiency | Ability to batch/prep and serve with a small team |
| Equipment burden | Required cooking, refrigeration, extraction and prep equipment |
| Ingredient overlap | Ability to reuse ingredients across menu items |
| Order value | Potential for profitable baskets and upsells |
| Repeat purchase | Likelihood of frequent repeat orders |
| Market demand | Evidence of customer demand in target markets |
| Competition | Ability to differentiate without excessive acquisition cost |
| Packaging | Suitability, cost and transport performance |
| Operational consistency | Ease of standardising recipes and QA |
| Franchise scalability | Ease of replicating the model across kitchens |
| Compliance complexity | Relative operational/regulatory complexity |
| Technology fit | Suitability for common ordering/KDS/inventory workflows |

## Decision rule

No concept is selected solely because it scores highest on demand. A candidate must demonstrate acceptable economics, delivery quality, operational repeatability and compliance feasibility.

The preferred pilot is the concept with the strongest combined commercial and operational case after evidence review, not necessarily the concept with the largest addressable market.

## Required evidence before selection

- competitor/menu scan
- indicative menu prices
- supplier/ingredient availability
- recipe-level cost assumptions
- packaging requirements
- production workflow
- estimated prep and cook times
- equipment implications
- delivery-quality test plan
- target customer definition
- repeat-purchase hypothesis
- initial unit-economics scenario

## Pilot gate

Concept selection remains `PENDING VALIDATION` until the evidence pack and unit-economics model have been reviewed together.
