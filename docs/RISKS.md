## Risk rules

- Critical risks require explicit mitigation before commercial launch.
- High risks must have an owner and next action before the relevant release gate.
- Unknown regulatory requirements must not be treated as resolved assumptions.
- Financial scenarios are planning tools, not earnings guarantees.
- AI must not bypass human, legal or food-safety controls.
