# Australian Packaging Cost Benchmarks — 2026-09-04

## Status
REFERENCE INPUTS / NOT SUPPLIER QUOTES / NOT LAUNCH APPROVAL

This document adds current public Australian packaging price references to the pilot menu and unit-economics evidence base. These figures are inputs for sensitivity modelling only. They must not be treated as contracted supplier costs, delivered costs, or final packaging selections.

## Evidence captured

### Burger packaging
- BE Food Services Queensland promotional pricing lists a soft-cardboard regular burger box at A$46.00 per 500, or A$0.092 each, excluding GST.
- Ecoware lists a brown corrugated burger box at A$66.90 per 250, or A$0.27 each, plus GST.
- Packery lists multiple Australian wholesale burger-box options, including A$0.17 each for a 500-pack kraft burger box and A$0.32 each for a 200-pack high burger box.
- Nisbets lists a compostable bagasse burger box at A$79.90 per 500, or A$0.16 each, ex GST.

### Meal / bowl packaging
- Nisbets lists a compostable hinged container at A$0.12 each for a 250-pack and a compostable hinged container at A$0.22 each for a 250-pack, ex GST, depending on size.
- Nisbets lists a 3-compartment sugarcane clamshell at A$0.53 each for a 200-pack, ex GST.
- Ecoware lists 16oz hinged rectangle containers at A$0.44 each and 24oz hinged rectangle containers at A$0.60 each on the captured page, plus GST.

### Ancillary packaging
- Packery's current Australian hospitality packaging index reports a median of A$0.300 for takeaway boxes/containers, A$0.316 for burger boxes/clamshells, A$0.208 for bowls/lids, A$0.128 for chip cups/trays, A$0.053 for bags, A$0.022 for napkins, and A$0.053 for cutlery/serving products.
- Ecoware lists burger holders at approximately A$0.09–A$0.10 each per 1,000 carton, plus GST.

## Modelling bands for the pilot

Use these only as temporary reference bands until a chosen menu, pack format, supplier, delivered freight and actual operating process are validated:

| Packaging component | Low reference | Base reference | High reference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Burger box | A$0.09 | A$0.27 | A$0.32 |
| Meal container | A$0.12 | A$0.30 | A$0.53 |
| Bag | A$0.05 | A$0.05 | A$0.10 |
| Napkin | A$0.02 | A$0.02 | A$0.04 |
| Cutlery/serving | A$0.05 | A$0.05 | A$0.10 |

These bands are deliberately conservative simplifications for scenario testing, not quotes. The high case should be replaced with the actual selected format where possible rather than used as an assumed permanent cost.

## Required validation before launch economics

For each representative SKU/bundle, capture:
1. exact packaging SKU and dimensions;
2. supplier and current quoted price;
3. carton quantity and minimum order;
4. freight/delivery treatment;
5. GST treatment;
6. suitability for the food, heat, moisture and delivery duration;
7. required bag, napkin, cutlery, sauce cup and label allocation;
8. waste/damage rate from pilot observation.

## Evidence boundary

Public wholesale/retail packaging prices are useful reference inputs but do not prove the Ghost Kitchen's final landed packaging cost. Product suitability, freight, supplier terms, volume discounts, GST and operational allocation remain to be validated.
