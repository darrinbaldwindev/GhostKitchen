## Critical boundary

These figures are **not** supplier quotes for the Ghost Kitchen project and must not be entered as contracted landed costs. Retail prices are not wholesale costs. Public foodservice prices may exclude delivery, account discounts, GST treatment, minimums or other commercial terms.

The A$5.22 burger benchmark is particularly useful as a sanity check, not as the project's recipe cost. The project still requires an actual recipe, gram weights, supplier/product identity, current supplier pricing, packaging allocation, labour/time study and delivery assumptions.

## Next costing capture

1. Representative SKU/bundle.
2. Recipe ingredient quantities.

5. Yield/waste assumption.
6. Packaging item and tested cost.
7. Prep/cook/assembly minutes.
8. Labour rate and labour/order.
9. Expected selling price/AOV hypothesis.
10. Delivery/channel/payment assumptions.
