## Purpose

Provide one deterministic capture format for the smallest representative menu set needed to feed the pilot unit-economics gate and concept scorecard.

This document is a capture template. Blank fields are intentionally unresolved. Do not replace missing evidence with estimates and do not present public benchmarks as contracted supplier/account terms.

## Candidate families

Capture at least three representative families from the current candidate set unless evidence justifies another set:

1. burgers
2. chicken
3. Asian street food
4. healthy/protein bowls
5. loaded fries/sides
6. dessert-focused concepts

Select three that provide useful operational contrast. Do not treat selection for testing as selection of the final concept.

## Evidence classes

- **VERIFIED** — directly observed, tested, or supported by an appropriate primary/account-specific source.
- **PARTIAL** — useful supporting evidence exists but a material uncertainty remains.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — planning assumption requiring testing.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence to score or cost confidently.

## Costing rules

### Ingredient cost

`ingredient cost per serving = purchase price ÷ purchase quantity × recipe quantity ÷ validated yield`

Where yield is not validated, keep the yield assumption explicitly marked **HYPOTHESIS** and do not upgrade the resulting cost to VERIFIED.

### Packaging

Use the actual packaging specification intended for the pilot where available. If a public catalogue benchmark is used, label it **PARTIAL/HYPOTHESIS** and replace it with an account-specific or tested cost before launch economics are approved.

### Labour

Record measured or defensible prep/cook/pack time separately from wage-rate assumptions. Do not assume owner labour is free. If a local award/classification or employment arrangement is required, flag it for professional/local confirmation.

### Contribution test inputs

Feed the evidence into the existing delivery-channel economics worksheet and unit-economics gate using separate fields for:

- selling price / AOV;
- ingredient cost;
- packaging;
- direct labour;
- payment cost;
- platform/channel commission;
- delivery cost or customer-delivery subsidy;
- acquisition/marketing cost;
- waste/remake/refund allowance;
- other directly attributable variable costs.

Do not calculate a final contribution margin when one or more material inputs are missing; show the missing input and keep the scenario status **NOT TESTABLE**.

## Delivery-test capture

For each candidate family, where practical, record:

- target delivery window;
- actual elapsed time;
- packaging used;
- product temperature/quality observation;
- leakage/crush/sogging or other packaging failure;
- remake/refund issue;
- reviewer/date;
- evidence reference.

Do not infer delivery performance from platform marketing claims alone.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. supplier/account-specific price lists, quotes or invoices;
2. directly observed/tested pilot measurements;
3. primary supplier or manufacturer documentation;
4. reputable public benchmarks;
5. secondary commentary only for discovery, never as sole evidence for a material commercial claim.

Record the capture date because prices, delivery terms and platform fees can change.

## Completion gate

The packet is **decision-ready** only when each finalist candidate has:

- representative recipes and quantities;
- traceable ingredient prices and yield treatment;
- packaging specification and cost;
- measured or defensible labour inputs;
- a clearly labelled selling-price/AOV hypothesis;
- delivery/channel assumptions with source references;
- enough evidence to run conservative/base/strong contribution scenarios;
- unresolved safety, compliance and operational questions explicitly listed.

A completed packet does **not** itself approve a concept. Final selection remains subject to the concept scorecard, unit-economics gate, delivery testing, safety/compliance review and project governance.
