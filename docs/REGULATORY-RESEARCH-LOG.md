# Ghost Kitchen Regulatory Research Log

**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Track authoritative regulatory evidence that can affect the pilot model. This is a research log, not legal advice.

## Queensland baseline research

- Queensland Government states that most food businesses need a licence from their local council, including businesses manufacturing food and retailing unpackaged food such as restaurants and takeaway businesses.
- Queensland Government states food businesses must comply with Food Safety Standard 3.2.3 requirements for premises and equipment, including suitability, cleanability, space, potable water, sewage, lighting, ventilation, hygiene facilities and pest protection.
- Queensland Government guidance on Standard 3.2.2A states specified category-one/category-two businesses require a certified food safety supervisor, food-handler training and evidence tools/record keeping.
- Queensland Government states food safety programs are mandatory for certain licensable businesses and recommends contacting the local council for business-specific requirements.
- Queensland Government delivery guidance states operators must maintain food safety during takeaway/delivery, including safe temperatures and prevention of cross-contamination.
