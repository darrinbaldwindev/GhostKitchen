# Ghost Kitchen Validation Gates

**Status:** Working release-control framework
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Gate 0 — Template definition

**Required:** common operating template documented and concept boundaries defined.

## Gate 1 — Concept selection

**Required:** candidate scorecard completed, evidence reviewed, risks assessed and pilot concept explicitly approved.

## Gate 2 — Kitchen feasibility

**Required:** kitchen model, equipment, workflow, storage and capacity validated for the selected concept and applicable requirements.

## Gate 3 — Menu economics

**Required:** recipe-level costing, packaging, waste and pricing assumptions validated.

## Gate 4 — Delivery validation

**Required:** packaging and product quality tested across the intended delivery window; dispatch and handoff workflow validated.

## Gate 5 — Operating repeatability

**Required:** SOPs, training, production timing, QA and exception handling demonstrated by more than one operator/test cycle where practical.

## Gate 6 — Unit economics

**Required:** conservative/base/strong model reconciled against pilot evidence, with break-even and sensitivity analysis.

## Gate 7 — Franchise readiness

**Required:** franchise package, support model, fees, standards and legal/professional review ready for formal assessment.

## Gate 8 — Commercial launch

**Required:** all critical risks addressed, evidence archived, launch checklist passed and explicit release decision recorded.

## Rule

Passing a gate means its stated evidence exists. A document marked “draft” does not constitute a passed gate.
