# Marketing Role & Next Actions

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Marketing requirements

- Define the primary customer and franchisee ICPs for the Ghost Kitchen Delivery Template.
- Validate the concept, menu/category proposition, local demand and competitive positioning.
- Build the customer acquisition funnel from discovery → menu/order → repeat purchase.
- Build the franchise recruitment funnel separately from customer marketing.
- Develop brand architecture for the central platform versus individual approved food concepts.
- Establish local SEO, delivery-market acquisition and retention loops.
- Validate unit economics before using pricing, margin or return claims in marketing.
- Create launch assets and operational marketing playbooks for the first kitchen/concept.

## Immediate action

Prioritise the single-kitchen/single-concept vertical slice. Validate customer demand, offer, acquisition economics and repeat-purchase mechanics before multi-brand expansion.

## Boundary
Food safety, legal, franchise and financial claims require independent evidence/authority; marketing cannot manufacture validation.
