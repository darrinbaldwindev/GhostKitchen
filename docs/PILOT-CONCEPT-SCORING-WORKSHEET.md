**Status:** Ready for evidence capture — no concept selected

## Purpose

Turn the existing concept scorecard into a repeatable decision worksheet. It is an evidence-capture tool, not evidence itself and not a forecast of franchise performance.

## Candidate set

Use the current research candidates unless new evidence justifies adding/removing a family:

- burgers
- chicken
- Asian street food
- healthy/protein bowls
- loaded fries/sides
- dessert-focused concepts

## Scoring protocol

For each candidate:

1. Score each criterion from 1–5 using the definitions in `docs/CONCEPT-SCORECARD.md`.
2. Record the evidence supporting the score.
3. Mark evidence as **VERIFIED**, **PARTIAL**, **HYPOTHESIS**, or **UNKNOWN**.
4. Calculate the weighted score only after all criteria have been reviewed.
5. Apply disqualifiers separately; a high weighted score cannot override a disqualifier.
6. Do not select a winner until economics and delivery testing are sufficiently evidenced.

## Evidence-quality rule

A score without evidence is a planning assumption. It must not be presented as a validated commercial result.

### Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by observed/tested evidence appropriate to the criterion |
| PARTIAL | Some supporting evidence exists, but important uncertainty remains |
| HYPOTHESIS | Reasonable working assumption requiring testing |
| UNKNOWN | Insufficient information to score confidently |

## Minimum evidence pack before selection

For each finalist, capture:

- representative menu and recipe assumptions;
- ingredient/supplier pricing;
- packaging specification and tested cost;
- prep and cook times;
- equipment list and capacity assumptions;
- delivery test at approximately 20, 30 and 40 minutes where practical;
- waste and remake observations;
- competitor/customer-demand evidence;
- AOV/bundle hypothesis;
- delivery/platform and payment cost assumptions;
- regulatory questions requiring local/professional confirmation.

## Decision gate

Do **not** select a pilot concept if:

- a critical safety/compliance question remains unresolved;
- delivery quality fails the intended service window;
- contribution economics are structurally weak;
- the concept cannot be standardised or trained efficiently;
- the apparent demand case depends mainly on unsupported assumptions.

When sufficient evidence exists, create a dated decision record identifying the selected concept, rejected alternatives, evidence relied upon, remaining risks and next validation step.

## Relationship to unit economics

This worksheet feeds `docs/UNIT-ECONOMICS-FRAMEWORK.md`. The selected finalist should not receive franchise pricing or earnings claims until recipe-level costing, delivery economics, labour, waste, refunds/cancellations and conservative/base/strong scenarios have been tested.
