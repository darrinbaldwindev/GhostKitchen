# Ghost Kitchen Missions

**Last updated:** 2026-08-31

## Mission status legend

- `QUEUED` — prioritised and awaiting execution
- `IN PROGRESS` — actively being worked
- `BLOCKED` — dependency prevents progress
- `PENDING VALIDATION` — draft work exists but evidence/approval is outstanding
- `COMPLETE` — acceptance criteria verified

## GK-P0-001 — Unit economics

**Status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Objective:** Build and validate Conservative/Base/Strong unit economics for Model B.  
**Acceptance:** Recipe, packaging, labour, kitchen, delivery/platform, payment, marketing and overhead assumptions represented; break-even and downside sensitivity calculated.  
**Dependency:** Pilot concept evidence.

## GK-P0-002 — Pilot concept selection

**Status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Objective:** Select one prepared-food concept for pilot development.  
**Acceptance:** Candidate evidence pack and scoring completed; concept selected only after joint review with unit economics.  
**Dependency:** Market and supplier research.

**Latest evidence:** `docs/PILOT-EVIDENCE-BASE-2026-08-31.md` establishes current market and Queensland compliance evidence and identifies remaining evidence gaps. Final selection remains deferred.

## GK-P0-004 — Candidate menu and recipe costing

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Build comparable representative SKUs and bundles for at least three leading candidate families.  
**Acceptance:** Ingredient quantities, dated supplier-price assumptions, food cost, packaging allocation, prep time, labour minutes and delivery resilience recorded.  
**Dependency:** Candidate shortlist.

## GK-P0-005 — Delivery-channel economics

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Model marketplace-heavy, hybrid and direct-order-led channel mixes, including free-delivery structures.  
**Acceptance:** Commission, payment, fulfilment, acquisition, retention and contribution economics are separately visible.  
**Dependency:** Representative menu/AOV assumptions.

## GK-P0-006 — Queensland pilot compliance pathway

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Confirm licence, food-safety-management, supervisor, premises and delivery requirements for the selected concept/location.  
**Acceptance:** Requirements are classified as confirmed, conditional or pending council/professional confirmation.  
**Dependency:** Concept and pilot location.

## GK-P0-003 — Pilot operating model

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Map order-to-repeat workflow for the selected concept.  
**Acceptance:** Owners, SOPs, KPIs, exception paths and QA controls defined for ordering, production, packaging, dispatch, delivery and retention.  
**Dependency:** Pilot concept selection.

## GK-P0-007 — Pilot test protocol

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Define controlled tests for food quality, delivery resilience, throughput, order accuracy, safety controls and economics.  
**Acceptance:** Dated test protocol, pass/fail thresholds, evidence capture and corrective-action loop exist before pilot launch.

## GK-P0-008 — Queensland pilot kitchen shortlist

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Identify suitable commercial/shared-kitchen options without committing to a lease.  
**Acceptance:** Occupancy model, equipment, storage, extraction, dispatch access, hours, delivery radius and council pathway compared.

## GK-P1-001 — Technology architecture

**Status:** QUEUED  
**Objective:** Define vendor-neutral system boundaries for ordering, payments, KDS, inventory, delivery, CRM, reporting and franchise management.  
**Dependency:** Pilot operating model.
