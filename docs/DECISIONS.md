# Ghost Kitchen Decisions

## D001 — Canonical repository

**Decision:** Use `darrinbaldwindev/GhostKitchen` as the canonical repository for the Ghost Kitchen project.

**Status:** Accepted

## D002 — Delivery-first model

**Decision:** The prepared-food franchise is delivery-first and does not depend on a customer-facing restaurant storefront.

**Status:** Accepted

## D003 — Template before brand proliferation

**Decision:** Build the Ghost Kitchen Delivery Template before attempting to launch multiple food concepts.

**Status:** Accepted

## D004 — Modular concept architecture

**Decision:** Food concepts are modules consisting of brand, menu, recipes, suppliers, packaging, pricing and marketing that plug into the common operating template.

**Status:** Accepted

## D005 — Commercial kitchen for prepared food

**Decision:** The initial prepared-food model targets an approved commercial kitchen. A home-kitchen model is not assumed.

**Status:** Accepted

## D006 — Validate economics before franchise pricing

**Decision:** Do not reuse Model A's franchise pricing or royalty assumptions for Model B. Build and validate the Model B unit economics first.

**Status:** Accepted

## D007 — Controlled multi-brand expansion

**Decision:** Multi-brand operation from one kitchen is a later-stage capability subject to franchisor/platform approval and demonstrated kitchen capacity.

**Status:** Accepted

## D008 — AI as oversight/support

**Decision:** AI/Overseer functions should assist, surface anomalies and recommend actions while preserving human/legal/food-safety controls and auditability.

**Status:** Accepted
